# Privacy Policy
Last updated: 3 October 2026

## 1. Who we are
This website is operated by the Entrepreneurship Club (E-Club), NIT Warangal ("we", "us"), a student club of the National Institute of Technology Warangal, Hanamkonda, Warangal, Telangana, India. Contact for privacy questions: [[NAME, ROLE]] at e_club@nitw.ac.in.

## 2. What personal data we collect
- **Data you send us:** if you email us or submit a form, we receive your name, email address, and the message you write. [[List exactly what each form collects.]]
- **Automatically:** basic technical data (IP address, browser, pages visited) processed by our hosting provider and cookieless analytics ([[Vercel Analytics / Plausible]]) to keep the site secure and understand usage. We do not use advertising trackers.
- We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 18. If you are under 18, please do not submit forms; use the linked event platform with a parent or guardian.

## 3. Why we use it
To reply to your message, to run and improve the site, to keep it secure, and — only if you ask — to send updates about club events. We do not sell your data.

## 4. Who else handles it
Hosting: [[Vercel Inc.]]. Forms: [[Google Forms / email provider]]. Event registration for competitions happens on third-party platforms such as Unstop, under their own privacy policies. These providers may process data outside India.

## 5. How long we keep it
Messages: [[12 months]] then deleted. Mailing-list details until you unsubscribe. Server logs: as per the provider's retention.

## 6. Your rights
You may ask us to access, correct or delete your personal data, withdraw consent, or make a complaint, by writing to the contact above. We will respond within [[30]] days. You may also approach the Data Protection Board of India as provided under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

## 7. Photos and videos
Event photos and videos may show attendees. If you appear in an image and want it removed, email us and we will remove it promptly.

## 8. Changes
We will post changes here with a new date.
