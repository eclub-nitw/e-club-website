**Why LCP is still 2.78 s on `/`.** The hero image is the LCP element and the real browser paints it at about 430 ms. Lighthouse's simulated throttling charges every byte that arrived before that paint (189 KB of framework JS, fonts, 18 KB hero) against 1.6 Mbps. Experiments (each measured, 3 runs): `experimental.inlineCss` made it **worse** (3.08 s, 420 KiB) and was reverted; dropping the JetBrains Mono file changed nothing (3.02 vs 3.00 s) and was reverted; shrinking the portrait hero from 40 KB to 18 KB gained 0.23 s (kept). The remaining cause is the React + Next baseline (172 KB gz on a bare app, measured in Phase 2), which no change here can remove.

**Known long task, not fixed.** About 0.96 s main-thread task on desktop about 2.5 s after load (scene-chunk evaluation plus WebGL context creation; the trace shows one `FunctionCall` in the React DOM chunk, no finer breakdown). It runs while the visitor is on the hero. Options if it matters: move the scene to a worker with `OffscreenCanvas`, or drop the eager mount and accept a stall at the first scene. Touch and low-end devices never load it.

### Judgment calls (every one)
1. **Photo sets are named "Club event 01/02/03"**, with months taken from each photo's camera date (Aug 2026 for the 29 Aug batch, Mar 2026 for the HEIC batch). Text visible inside photos ("The Pitch League" on a board, "Pitch'er Perfect" on a slide) was NOT used as an event name.
2. **HEIC decoding**: sharp cannot decode HEVC here, so Windows WIC converted the 28 HEIC files (27 OK, `IMG_5602` undecodable). Converted JPEGs live in `raw-media/_heic` (git-ignored).
3. **Soft-focus cut-off at sharpness 160** removed 7 frames; the 2 "ai_select" files are phone screenshots, excluded. List in `MEDIA-INVENTORY.md`. Nothing was excluded for privacy; one wide gallery-only frame (`03-39`, student in a checked dress in the foreground) deserves a glance.
4. **Font**: Bricolage axes `opsz 12-96, wght 200-800, wdth 75-100` are all exposed by the full variable file (from `google/fonts`). Shipped: `opsz` 96, `wght` 600-800, **`wdth` pinned at 75** (20 KB). The two-axis cut is 42 KB (estimated +0.1 s simulated LCP; not measured). Display type is therefore condensed everywhere, including paper sections; sentence case vs uppercase is the only split.
5. **Wordmark fills**: the letters are `background-clip: text` windows onto an ungraded 1024 px copy of the hero photo (loaded at idle, skipped on Save-Data). The pointer moves a light over them (transform only) rather than a true pointer-following mask; touch gets one automatic sweep.
6. **New token `club-ember #ff9147`** (7.4:1 on ink) for manifesto emphasis because `club-gold` on ink is 6.8:1, below the 7:1 manifesto rule. Unlit manifesto lines are the muted token (7.9:1), lit lines near-white (14:1). Recorded in `tokens.css`.
7. **No WebGL literal from tokens**: the Dive scene uses `#0b2226` literally for fog/background (WebGL needs a value; the scene is always on the ink theme).
8. **Two WebGL contexts on desktop Home** (Dive + vortex portal), never more; `qa.mjs` counts canvases during a full scroll (max 2). The coin lives inside the portal, so no third context.
9. **Dive fallback shows 8 of 13 photographs** in the stack (less scrolling on phones) with a link to the gallery.
10. **"Moments" rail cursor label is `SCROLL`, not `DRAG`**: on desktop the rail is scroll-scrubbed and pinned, so dragging would mislead. Touch swipes natively.
11. **Partner marquee and team/partner blocks stay hidden** with no consented data; the marquee was tested with a temporary four-logo fixture (speed 1x to 6x on scroll, decays to about 1x), then the fixture was removed.
12. **Counting up** applies only to real counts (`₹50,000`); `30–31 Oct` and `2006` are shown as written.
13. **Team page header photo** is an event photo under the heading "The people behind the club" while no roster exists; it may read as "these are the team". Swap or remove if that is a worry.
14. **Stats strip** shows three facts: prize pool and finale dates (from `CONTEXT.md`) and **Technozion established 2006** (public NITW brochure, marked CONFIRM). E-Summit '25 is not attributed to E-Club.
15. **Pull-quotes are unsigned club-voice lines** ("E-Club"), not attributed to people, all CONFIRM. The UI uses option 0 of each list in `src/data/copy.ts`.
16. **LCP levers**: `inlineCss` tried and reverted (worse); JetBrains Mono removal tried and reverted (no gain); `images.qualities` not needed (hero uses pre-made files, not the optimizer). `next.config.ts` ends unchanged.
17. **drei removed from scene imports** (plain `GLTFLoader` and `TextureLoader`): no size change, fewer surprises. `@react-three/drei` stays in `package.json` (unused now; removing it is a dependency decision for you). `useGLTF` had silently attached a Meshopt wasm decoder that the CSP blocks, which is why the loader was made explicit.
18. **Shader pre-compile** (`compileAsync`) and staged texture upload removed a 134-164 ms task at the Dive entrance.
19. **Screenshots (43 MB) are committed** in `docs/handoff/v3/`; drop them from the branch before any merge if repo size matters.
20. Gallery thumbs are `fetchpriority=low` and the lightbox image is not rendered until first open (a closed dialog had been fetching three full-size images).

### What still looks weak (candid)
- **The photographs.** They are candid classroom phone shots: ultra-wide distortion, beige walls, tube light, cluttered desks. The grade, crop and motion make them usable, but the moments rail and gallery show them close to raw and they read as documentation, not as a brand. Better photography, even 10 well-lit frames, would lift the whole site more than any further effect.
- In the Dive, planes of flat, bright classroom photos against dark fog look a little like a slideshow in a tunnel; the depth is real but the content is not cinematic.
- LCP (see above) does not meet 2.5 s in Lighthouse's simulated profile.
- The coin reads a little dull in the live scene compared with the Cycles poster (environment reflections only, no HDRI).
- Copy is ours and minimal; until `CONTENT-INTAKE.md` is filled the site has almost no club voice.
- Inner pages (About, Team, Sponsors, Join, Contact) are photo headers over very little content; they are honest, not rich.
