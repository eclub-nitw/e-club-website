**Why LCP is still above 2.5 s.** On the cover the LCP element is the h1 text (Lighthouse names `h1.t-h1`); in a real browser it paints in about 0.9 s, but the simulated throttle charges the Next + React baseline (about 172 KB gz of unavoidable JS) and the 70 KB of fonts against 1.6 Mbps. Experiments: `font-display: optional` on Bricolage (no gain, reverted), eager first gallery thumbnails (no gain, kept, harmless). Dropping the `wdth` axis would save about 28 KB of font and is the next lever if the owner wants the number over the typography. The Gallery misses perf because it is 41 photographs on one page; a paginated gallery would fix it.

### Judgment calls (every one)
1. **Cut the Dive** (above). Chapter numbering and the counter therefore count 9 slides.
2. **Poster = Blender render**, not the generated hero-poster, because the live scene must match it; the poster background is the exact page ink so no scrim or seam is needed. The live composition and the poster are close but **not identical** (lighting differs slightly; the live scene has no true reflection, only 5% mirrored ghosts). The poster fades out when the live assets have loaded and the bars then rise.
3. **Live scene only >= 1024 px.** Tablets and phones keep the poster (mobile uses a portrait render with a spacer so the bars show between the buttons and the countdown).
4. **Cover h1 is a readable Headline-voice line**; the giant "E-CLUB" wordmark is decoration (generated content, low opacity, bottom edge) so the Impact voice appears once and contrast checks do not trip on a 14% wordmark.
5. **Traction is an explicit illustration**: bars with no axis or numbers labelled "Illustration. Not data."; the only figures are verified facts with sources (prize pool, finale dates, Technozion 2006 marked CONFIRM). Never implies growth.
6. **Investors with no consented partners** shows an honest "No partners are listed yet" slide with an invitation instead of an empty marquee; the marquee component renders as soon as a consented sponsor exists.
7. **Sponsors tiers** ("Flagship / Event / Community partner") are drafted structure and flagged CONFIRM (COPY-REVIEW row 17): the club has not defined tiers.
8. **Solution areas** reuse one verified row (Competitions / Venture Vortex) and two inferred from the photo archive; flagged CONFIRM and shown on About only until `about.verticals` is supplied.
9. **Event covers are generated art** (hover-swap pairs), labelled neutrally "Club event 01..03 / Photo set", linking to the Gallery section. Art is never presented as a photograph.
10. **Moments choice** (by sharpness, level horizon, readable subject, nothing awkward): 02-22, 01-15, 01-13, 01-03, 02-21, 03-33, 01-07, 01-09. Excluded: 03-39 (student in a checked dress in the foreground), 01-10 (close-up of a seated student), 02-23 (near-duplicate). Several frames show a slide or board with text ("The Pitch League", "Entropia"); the duotone makes it unreadable at card size but it is still in the files. No faces are named or guessed.
11. **Contact/Join form has no backend** (out of scope): on a valid submit it validates, then opens the visitor's own email app with the message pre-filled and says so; nothing is stored or sent by us. zod was replaced by 12 lines of validation after it added about 90 KB gz to those two routes. `zod` and `@react-three/drei` are now unused in `package.json`; removing them is your call.
12. **Weight 500 mono and weight 400 sans only**: Instrument Sans ships at 400, so there is no bold body text; the legal pages' `**bold**` renders as full-colour underlined text instead of a faked bold.
13. **`font-variation-settings` for width**: `next/font/local` cannot declare a `font-stretch` range, so `wdth` is set per voice in CSS.
14. **Tokens**: new `--color-club-orange-ink #a14e00` (5.2:1 on paper) for small accent text on paper; grain 4% to 3.5%. Both in `tokens.css`, logged here.
15. **Nav**: single floating pill instead of the V3 full-header-plus-pill pair; pill shape kept for the nav and the flagship button only (everything else is radius <= 2px).
16. **Old `scripts/qa.mjs` (V3) deleted**: it waited on elements that no longer exist and hung. `scripts/qa-v4.mjs` replaces it.
17. **`/states`** (every voice and component state, for the states-sheet screenshot) is a 404 unless built with `SHOW_STATES=1`; the final build and all audits above were run without it.
18. **Cartier Watches & Wonders** returned Access Denied to automation, so it is not in the research note; 9 sites were opened and audited (`docs/RESEARCH-V4.md`).
19. **Screenshots (about 40 MB) are committed** in `docs/handoff/v4/`; drop them before any merge if repo size matters. Before/after sheet: `docs/handoff/v4/BEFORE-AFTER-1440.jpg`.
20. The 3D vortex portal keeps its own WebGL context (so up to 2 canvases alive on Home: cover + portal, never in view together); particles halved to 1,700 and a lit pedestal added under the coin.

### Candid weak points
- LCP and some perf scores are below target, as above. `/` is exactly on 90.
- The live Rising Ledger is good but not photographic: flatter than the Blender poster, and the hand-off from poster to live scene is a cross-fade between two similar-but-not-identical images. On a slow GPU the visitor sees only the poster, which is the better-looking of the two.
- Almost all copy is CONFIRM draft written without club input (`COPY-REVIEW.md`, 17 rows). The Problem/Solution slides read well but are not the club's voice yet.
- The Moments frames are still classroom phone photos; the duotone helps a lot but cannot make them professional. Real event photography or the owner's best frames would raise this chapter the most.
- Not tested on real phones, Safari, Firefox or a screen reader. Touch and reduced-motion layouts were screenshotted and audited, not hand-held.
- The ledger hairlines draw with CSS scroll-driven animation: Firefox and older Safari show them fully drawn (by design, not animated).
