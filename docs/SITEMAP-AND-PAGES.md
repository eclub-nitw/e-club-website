## Navigation
Header: Home · About · Events · Team · Sponsors · Gallery · Contact + gold button "Venture Vortex 2026". Footer: sitemap columns, legal links, email, socials, "Made by the E-Club tech team", © year.

## Data model (typed files in `src/data/`)
`site.ts` (name, url, email, socials), `events.ts` (Event[]: slug, title, type, dateStart, dateEnd, venue, summary, stats, coverImage, gallery[], videoId?, sponsors[], registerUrl?), `team.ts` (Member[]: name, role, group, year, photo, linkedin?), `sponsors.ts`. Media in `public/images/{events,team,sponsors}/…`, videos on the club's YouTube channel (embed with youtube-nocookie facade). NEVER embed Google Drive videos (slow, quota limits, not indexable). Photos: originals stay on Drive; commit compressed WebP/AVIF ≤ 1600px wide (`scripts/optimize-images.mjs` with `sharp`).

## Forms (phase 1 → 2)
Phase 1: `mailto:` + Google Form links (Google is the data processor; state it in the Privacy Policy). Phase 2: a Next.js route handler that emails the club through a transactional email API, stores nothing, has a honeypot field and rate limiting.

## Merge plan for Venture Vortex
The VV repo exports `<VentureVortexPage />` from `src/features/venture-vortex/`. To merge: copy that folder to `src/features/venture-vortex/`, copy `src/data/event.ts` and `startups.ts` to `src/data/venture-vortex/`, create `src/app/venture-vortex/page.tsx` that renders it and exports its metadata + JSON-LD, add the nav link, and 301-redirect the old Vercel URL to `/venture-vortex` (or keep it as a subdomain). Tokens are already shared, so no CSS conflicts.
